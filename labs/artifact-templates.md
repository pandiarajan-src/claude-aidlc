# Lab artifact templates

Copy the relevant headings into separate files in your practice repository.
Fill them in as each step is reviewed, not all at once.

## intent.md

- User and problem:
- Outcome and acceptance criteria (assign IDs):
- Constraints and non-goals:
- Open questions and owner:
- Reviewed by / decision:

## spec.md

- Source intent and acceptance criteria:
- Interface, inputs, outputs, and failures:
- Alternatives and why we chose this one:
- Compatibility or migration:
- Evidence needed before release:
- Reviewed by / decision:

## plan.md

- Source spec:
- Smallest end-to-end change and files:
- Tests to add or preserve:
- Commands to verify:
- Risks, worktree if needed, and rollback:
- Reviewed by / decision:

## release.md

- Reviewed candidate commit and PR (or local review record):
- Acceptance IDs, test commands, and observed results:
- Simulated target and smoke checks:
- Rollback trigger, action, and recovery evidence:
- Approval or hold decision:

## incident.md

- Observations, timestamps, and candidate revision:
- Hypotheses and missing evidence:
- Approved mitigation and result:
- Follow-up intent, success criterion, and owner:
